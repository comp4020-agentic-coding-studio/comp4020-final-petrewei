Fridge Poetry

What this is for

One fridge door, one set of words, everyone at once. Build a line of three words or more: it scores while it stands, double if it answers the prompt. Every word exists once, so steal theirs, and guard yours.

  • Drag a word to move it
  • Or focus it and press ← ↑ → ↓, with Shift for bigger steps
