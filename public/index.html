Fridge Poetry

One fridge door, shared with everyone who visits. Every word exists once: if it's in someone's line, it isn't in yours. Drag a word, or focus it and use the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps). Words you moved last are outlined, and stay where you left them.

What this is for

Each word is a button. Use the arrow keys to move the focused word, and Shift with an arrow key to move it further.
