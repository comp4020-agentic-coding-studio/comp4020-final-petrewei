# ADR 0083: Settings, a Picture of the Round, and a Candle's Reading

Status: accepted, 5 November 2026. From the demo games, made the game's own.

## 1 Decision

**Settings.** A gear opens a dialog of switches kept in this browser: sound effects, off until chosen, made in the page with no files, for your own fills, wins, calls and cuts; a calm screen, which applies the page's own reduced-motion rules on demand; and music and the dark theme. Music and the theme moved there from the market's header, so the header keeps its width with the new medal and gear and its layout check holds; the About page keeps its own buttons. Music still starts off and never loads its track by itself (ADR 0020).

**A picture of the round.** At the bell, a player who traded may save an image: the result as a percentage, the score and rank, what the round was now that it is revealed, and the account through the round's closed trades as a line. It is drawn in the page from what it already has and saved to the player's device; nothing is sent, and a replay's dates appear only after the bell.

**A candle's reading.** A pointer over the chart shows the candle under it: open, high, low, close and its change, in a box beside the pointer. The draw keeps each candle's centre and prices, so moving the pointer never redraws the chart.
