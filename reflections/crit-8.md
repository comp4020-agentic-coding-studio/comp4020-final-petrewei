# Crit 8 Reflection

## 1 What Was the Breakthrough That Moved the Work Forward?

Changing what the ideas were judged against. With two hours left, the first comparison asked what could be live by the cutoff, and it picked a shared lamp because it was small. When I said the final artefact mattered more than tonight, the comparison was rerun against the final project's marking and the crit 9 and 10 specs: ten minutes of a marker's use, a real decision about two people acting at once, and logs that can tell a story. The lamp went from first to last, and Fridge Poetry won because its hard parts, two people wanting the same word and moving words without a mouse, are exactly what the later crits ask me to write about.

## 2 What Did This Work Change About Who I Want to Be as a Software Developer?

I let the deadline set the question at first, and that would have given me an easy app that could not carry five weeks of work. I want to choose the criteria before the candidates, because the ranking follows from them. I also rejected the agent's step sequencer even though it scored well, because it repeated my crit 4 instrument; that judgement was about what I wanted to learn, which no ranking could make for me.
