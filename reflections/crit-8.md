# Crit 8 Reflection

## 1 What Was the Breakthrough That Moved the Work Forward?

Treating the choice of app as a judgement about what good means, rather than a question of what could be live by the cutoff. With two hours left, the first comparison ranked ideas by how small they were and put a shared lamp first. When I said the final artefact mattered more than tonight, the comparison was rerun against criteria taken from the brief and the later crits: a marker using the app for ten minutes, a real decision about several people acting at once, and logs that can tell a story. The lamp dropped to last. Fridge Poetry won because its central rule, that each word exists once, could be stated in `README.md`, kept as a rule in `CLAUDE.md` and checked in `spec/fridge.test.ts`, so the argument, the rules and the checks say the same thing. Its persistence is server-side: positions live in a file on the `/data` volume, and a word I placed survived every redeploy that morning.

## 2 What Did This Work Change About Who I Want to Be as a Software Developer?

I want my contribution to be choosing and revising the criteria, and directing the agent towards them. The agent compared about twenty-five options, but the decisions were mine: valuing the final artefact over the cutoff, and rejecting the step sequencer it recommended because it repeated my crit 4 instrument. Letting the agent compare without choosing, then making the choice myself, kept the reasons visible, and ADR 0001 records the stack choice the same way. The agent also drafted this repository's first `README.md` and `PROCESS.md`, and my remaining work is to review those drafts for a faithful account of my actual reasoning, not to accept them because they read well.
