# Process Overview

## 1 From the Brief to a Game

The brief asks for a multi-user, real-time website that is good, leaving "good" to me. For crits 8 and 9 the app was Fridge Poetry, a shared door of word magnets, each word once ([ADR 0001](docs/adr/0001-plain-node-and-a-json-file.md) to [0008](docs/adr/0008-rounds-with-a-prompt.md)). After crit 9 I judged it unplayable. Scored rounds ([`4c5f328`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-final-petrewei/commit/4c5f328)) did not change that, nor did a letter race on a branch ([`c6fa7a1`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-final-petrewei/commit/c6fa7a1)): I don't find word games fun.

I had it read 125 classmates' projects; co-op, racing and cards were crowded. I named strategy games and live markets. I then proposed a shared foreign-exchange market on real or simulated prices, where players compete by prediction and leverage, parodying *FX Fighter Kurumi-chan*. I asked for mainstream currencies and hidden-date crisis replays. I played the prototype, liked it, and replaced the fridge ([`66449d4`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-final-petrewei/commit/66449d4), [ADR 0009](docs/adr/0009-a-parody-fx-trading-floor.md)), and named it Losscut! Kurumi's FX Fantasy.

## 2 Stack and Harness

The stack outlived the switch: Node, server-sent events, Fly ([ADR 0001](docs/adr/0001-plain-node-and-a-json-file.md)). One process owns price, accounts, clock.

The harness has three parts. `README.md` argues what good means and which claims are enforced. I had `CLAUDE.md` cut to rules alone ([`4269d0a`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-final-petrewei/commit/4269d0a)), as facts belong in the README and brief; the switch added theme rules: play money, no official art, satire of habits. `spec/` checks the running app; `test/` drives rules tick by tick. Tests preceded `src/fx.ts` ([`8c98e74`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-final-petrewei/commit/8c98e74)).
